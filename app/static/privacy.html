Privacy Policy

Infinity Agent, operated by OneInfinity LLC · Last updated September 10, 2026

This policy explains what information Infinity Agent ("we," "us") collects when a business connects their Google account, how we use it, and what choices you have. It applies to the Infinity Agent web application.

Information we collect

  • Your Google account profile: the email address associated with the Google account you connect, used to identify your account with us.
  • Gmail message content: the sender, subject, and body of messages in your Gmail inbox, read in order to identify messages that may need a reply and to draft a suggested response.
  • Calendar availability: free/busy time blocks from your Google Calendar, read in order to suggest meeting times in scheduling-related drafts. We do not read event titles, descriptions, or attendees, and we do not create or modify calendar events.
  • OAuth credentials: the access and refresh tokens Google issues when you connect your account, stored so we can continue acting on your behalf until you disconnect.
  • Usage data: records of drafts generated, and whether you approved, edited, or rejected them, used to measure and improve the service.

How we use this information

We use the information above solely to operate Infinity Agent for your account: identifying messages that may need a reply, drafting a suggested response using an AI language model, and — only once you review and approve, edit-and-approve, or reject the draft yourself — sending the approved reply through your connected Gmail account. Nothing is ever sent without your explicit approval.

AI processing

Message content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate a draft reply or scheduling proposal. Anthropic processes this content under its own API terms and does not use it to train models. We do not send your data to any other AI provider.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We share information only with:

  • Google (to read/send Gmail and read Calendar availability, as authorized by you),
  • Anthropic (to generate draft text, as described above), and
  • our infrastructure providers (hosting, database, and cache providers) solely to operate the service, under their own confidentiality and security commitments.

Data retention and deletion

We retain message content and drafts for as long as your account is connected, so you can review your history and so we can measure the service's accuracy over time. You can revoke Infinity Agent's access at any time from your Google Account security settings. To request deletion of your data, email us at jefftian.oneinfinity@gmail.com and we will delete it within a reasonable time.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to stored data is limited to the systems and personnel that need it to operate the service. We are in the process of adding encryption at rest for stored Google credentials as we move beyond initial pilot use.

Your choices

You control access at all times: disconnect Infinity Agent from your Google Account permissions page, and approval is always required before any reply is sent under your name.

Children's privacy

Infinity Agent is a business tool not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify connected accounts by email before the changes take effect.
